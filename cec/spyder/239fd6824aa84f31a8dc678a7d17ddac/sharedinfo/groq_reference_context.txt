SECTION: East Point Medical College overview

SECTION: Departments
Anatomy
The Anatomy department of the college has various collections of species and human body parts like ( brain ,lungs ,tongue ,face etc.) for study purposes . In fact , this is the first thing you do after joining college in the first year . department also has skulls and bones of the body ,charts and models for better learning . department also have microscopes and histology slides.
Physiology
Physiology is one of the first three practical in MBBS(first year)and physiology is a branch of biology that deals with normal functions of the human body and life, basic things that you will need for life will thought in this lab like measurement of blood pressure ,pulse rate, hematology test. lab of physiology in east point medical college is well equipped and four practical halls just for physiology with modern microscopes and equipment for practical.
Biochemistry
Biochemistry is a science that deals with chemical and physio-chemical processes and substances that occur within living organisms. teachers in biochemistry lab will teach you about chemicals and experiments like differential tests for blood sugars ,chemical examination of normal urine, test for jaundice( liver function test). Lab in east point medical college is huge and has a single hall which can accommodate 100 students. Lab is facilitated with all the equipment and audio – visual smart screens.
Pathology
Pathology is a branch of science that deals with study of disease and diagnosis through removed tissue. lab in east point medical college of pathology is equipped with modern diagnostic aids for investigations and hematology instruments. department has a museum that has a collection of various number of spices of diseased organs and chart and models for better understanding. Pathology lab also has around 100 microscopes for studying slides.
Microbiology
Microbiology is a branch of science that deals with study of microorganisms and morphology of the microorganisms. lab has one single hall that can accommodate 75 students staining.
Pharmacology
Pharmacology is a branch of medical science that deals with the study of drugs and its mechanism. Pharmacology is one of the interesting practicals of MBBS. Lab is totally equipped for drug preparation. Equipment used in college are verified and safe.
Forensic Medicine
The department teaches the legal aspect of medical practice to students that helps in resolution of criminal cases on the basis of scientific facts. The department has access to laboratories and seminar rooms. For forensic study the lab is facilitated with microscope ,x-ray machines. Age estimation is learnt by x -rays and the main aspect that help in study of the criminal case is dentition of the criminal ,bite marks and lip bite. tooth molds and OPG are given for studies. Department also has a museum that has a collection of various bones, forensic cases and photographs, snake specimens and different types of weapons.
Community Medicine
Community medicine department is all about serving the rural population by offering them medical service. The department of east point medical college conducts at least two camps every week .The department is equipped with smart TVs for displaying content in the form of videos and pictures for better understanding of students and patients. Department also has computers for statistical cells, community medicine has a number of charts ,posters and survey cases for better understanding about health issues and hygiene importance and educating patients for the same.

➤ classrooms – The college has 4 theaters with light and ventilation. Classrooms have centralized air conditioners ,smart class, white and green board, metallic chairs and table. There are separate demo classes in every department with basic white boards and chairs.
➤ laboratories – The college has separate labs for separate departments which are also very ventilated and hygienic. All the safety measures have been taken in construction and maintenance .There are well equipped and students are provided with all the materials for experiments and practical.
