SECTION: Is there an internship program at Uttaranchal Dental College Dehradun?
Yes, the college offers internships to students who have completed a four-year BDS degree program. Furthermore, the college provides a stipend to students during the internship period.

SECTION: What is Uttaranchal Dental College Dehradun’s annual BDS and MDS intake?
The number of seats available for BDS is 100 per academic year, while the number of seats available for MDS is 9 per academic year, including all specializations.

SECTION: Which university is Uttaranchal Dental College Dehradun affiliated with?
Uttaranchal Dental College Dehradun is affiliated with the Hemwati Nandan Bahuguna University in Srinagar, Garhwal, and Uttarakhand.

SECTION: Is there a hospital attached to the Uttaranchal Dental College Dehradun?
To serve the patients, the college has a hospital named Uttaranchal Hospital, which has a fully functional OPD and a large patient inflow daily, as well as well-equipped clinical labs and many other amenities.

SECTION: How many specializations are available for Masters of Dental Surgery students at the college?
Uttaranchal Dental College offers a total of five specializations for the Master of Dental Surgery degree.

SECTION: What are the various career options available after graduating from Uttaranchal Dental College Dehradun?
After BDS, you can pursue a Master of Dental Surgery, practice dentistry, or work in a government dental hospital, among other options.

SECTION: What is Uttaranchal Dental College fee structure for BDS?
Uttaranchal Dental College fee structure for BDS is INR 2,15,000/-.

SECTION: What is Uttaranchal Dental College fee structure for MDS?
Uttaranchal Dental College fee structure for MDS is INR 4,00,000/- to INR 7,09,000/- per year.
